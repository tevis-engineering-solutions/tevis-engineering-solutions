1. Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. Tevis Engineering Solutions collects only the information necessary to deliver services, respond to inquiries, and process payments. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

1.1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us, submit a support ticket, or engage our services:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and physical address (for on-site services).
  • Device & Technical Information: Descriptions of hardware, software, operating systems, and network configurations you provide in support requests.
  • Payment Information: Invoice identifiers and payment confirmation records. Credit card and payment data is processed directly by PayPal — we do not store card numbers or sensitive payment credentials.
  • Communications: Email correspondence, ticket submissions, and notes from service sessions, retained for record-keeping and future reference.
  • Website Usage Data: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and analytics purposes. We do not use tracking cookies beyond what GitHub Pages may set by default.

1.2 How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, deliver, and follow up on IT support and engineering services.
  • To generate invoices and process payments.
  • To respond to support tickets and inquiries.
  • To maintain service history for repeat clients.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

1.3 Data Storage & Security

Your data may be stored in:

  • Email: Communications via Gmail / ProtonMail are subject to those providers' privacy policies.
  • ProtonDrive: Service records and invoices may be stored in encrypted cloud storage.
  • Formspree: Support ticket and contact form submissions are processed by Formspree, Inc. See formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • PayPal: Payment processing is handled by PayPal, Inc. See paypal.com privacy policy.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data, but no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You use our services at your own risk with respect to data transmission security.

1.4 Data Retention

We retain service records, communications, and invoices for a minimum of 3 years for accounting and business continuity purposes. You may request deletion of your personal information by contacting us directly, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations.

1.5 Third-Party Services

Our website and tools may link to or integrate with third-party services (PayPal, Formspree, Google Fonts, Font Awesome, GitHub Pages, Chart.js). These services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their data practices.

1.6 Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

To exercise these rights, contact us at tyler@tevisengineering.com.

1.7 Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

1.8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any updated policy.
