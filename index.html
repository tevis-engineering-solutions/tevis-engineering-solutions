Service Agreement Highlights

Deposits and Milestones

Projects up to $5,000 use 50% kickoff and 50% delivery billing. Larger projects use milestone billing (40% kickoff, 30% midpoint, 30% delivery).

Payment Terms

Weekly invoices are net 7 days. Monthly support invoices are net 15 days. Project milestone invoices are due on issue.

Late Payments

Friendly reminder at 5 days overdue. Balances beyond the due date accrue a 1.5% monthly finance charge (18% APR). Service suspended on accounts 14+ days past due without prior arrangement.

Monthly Hard Cap Rule

Monthly support includes up to 24 billable hours with availability capped at 28 billable hours. Hours 25-28 require written approval and are billed at the standard overage rate of $100/hour.

Scope Change Process

New requirements are handled through a written change order showing added scope, estimated effort, and updated price before work begins.

Support vs Engineering

Support plans cover maintenance/admin operations. Engineering design, custom build/test, and formal deliverables require a separate project SOW.
